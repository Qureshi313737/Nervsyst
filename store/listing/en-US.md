## Full description (max 4000)
Neuromap by Lumifya turns the human nervous system into a living 3D atlas you can explore, question and learn from, completely offline.

Rotate a full-body model and tap any nerve, any part of the brain or any spinal segment. A clear study card opens with its origin, course, motor and sensory supply, branches, clinical correlation, how it is examined and a memory aid. Every fact is checked against standard textbooks, and the sources are listed inside the app.

WHAT YOU CAN EXPLORE
• Brain: cerebral lobes (tap a lobe to identify it), cerebellum, midbrain, pons and medulla
• Deep brain: thalamus, hypothalamus and pituitary, corpus callosum, basal ganglia, hippocampus, amygdala
• Spinal cord with all 31 pairs of spinal nerves, dorsal root ganglia and the cauda equina
• All 12 cranial nerves, including the three trigeminal divisions, the five facial branches and the recurrent laryngeal nerves
• Cervical, brachial, lumbar and sacral plexuses: roots, trunks, divisions, cords and every major branch
• Intercostal nerves, the sympathetic chain, splanchnic nerves and the vagus

CINEMATIC PATHWAY TOURS
Watch a glowing signal travel real routes while the camera follows it:
• The knee-jerk reflex arc
• Voluntary movement: why the left brain moves the right hand
• Pain and temperature (spinothalamic tract)
• Fine touch and vibration (dorsal columns)
• The visual pathway and the optic chiasm
• Fight-or-flight vs rest-and-digest

FROM BODY TO NEURON
Zoom from the whole body into a peripheral nerve (epineurium, perineurium, fascicles, myelin and nodes of Ranvier) and watch saltatory conduction. Then follow a motor neuron to the neuromuscular junction as acetylcholine is released and the muscle contracts.

LEARN AT YOUR LEVEL
• Basic: plain-language explanations
• Standard: the detail you need for medical and nursing exams
• Advanced: anatomical variations, embryology and references

THREE LANGUAGES
English, हिन्दी and اردو for the interface, explanations, tours and quizzes.

TEST YOURSELF
A quick quiz highlights a structure and asks you to name it, with score and streak.

BUILT FOR EVERY PHONE
Neuromap measures your device and adjusts its quality automatically, so animations stay smooth on budget phones and look stunning on flagships. No account, no ads, no tracking, and it works without internet.

Colour modes (by system, by function, or classic atlas yellow), filters by plexus, function and region, labels, search and a direction-aware signal animation (motor outward, sensory inward) make Neuromap a complete companion for students of medicine, nursing, physiotherapy, dentistry and biology, and for anyone curious about how the body is wired.

Neuromap is an educational tool. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.
