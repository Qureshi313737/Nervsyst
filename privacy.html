Neuromap Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026 · Applies to the Neuromap Android app (package com.lumifya.neuromap) and the Neuromap website.

In short: Neuromap does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. The Android app asks for no permissions and works completely offline.

Who we are

Neuromap is an educational 3D atlas of the human nervous system, published by Lumifya ("we").

Information we collect

None. Neuromap does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information, and it contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software.

Information stored on your device

To remember your choices, the app saves a few settings in your device's local storage: language, learning level, selected filter, colour mode, display toggles and quality setting. These stay on your device, are never sent to us or anyone else, and are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Permissions and network access

The Android app requests no runtime permissions and does not use the internet: all 3D models, text and fonts are bundled inside the app. If you open an external link (for example a reference), it opens in your own browser under that browser's privacy policy.

The website version

When you use Neuromap in a web browser, the page is served by GitHub Pages and loads the three.js library from the jsDelivr CDN and fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web server, these providers receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type in order to deliver files, under their own privacy policies. We do not receive or store this information.

Children

Neuromap is a general educational app. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Lumifya through the developer contact email shown on the Neuromap listing in Google Play.
